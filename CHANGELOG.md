# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-04

- First preview with iCloud, Gmail, Yahoo, AOL, 163, 126, QQ and custom IMAP over TLS presets and provider-specific sign-in help. OAuth providers, including Outlook.com and Exchange Online, are deferred.
- Complete English and Simplified Chinese interfaces, help, errors and documentation; follows KOReader's language setting.
- Focused setup, manual refresh, compact pagination, individual and page selection, background downloads, cancellation and retries.
- Encoded filenames, collision-safe storage and incremental checks; preserves messages and their read status. Downloaded books open in KOReader.
- Translation checks, native integration and automatic packaging and releases. Local TLS and headless native tests cover both languages; real providers, physical devices and manual touch interaction remain unverified.
